Renewals, notices, and disputes — your matters are already sorted, timed, and waiting.

Your first 5 minutes

  1. Open Legal Portal.
  2. The Renewal monitor sorts every lease by how soon it ends — top of the list needs you first.
  3. Open the lease timeline: estates as swimlanes, leases as bars. See conflicts and clusters instantly.
  4. Check the case board — disputes and notice work, by stage.

A normal day for you

  1. Work the renewal monitor top-down: critical (≤30 days) first.
  2. Draft notices from your templates; log each step on the matter's task so history is automatic.
  3. For disputes (like a tenant in arrears under dispute), the balance is held separately — it never quietly disappears.

Your weekly rhythm

Weekly: scan the 90-day window for leases about to enter notice territory and open tasks now.
