Onboarding — The Principal

Day-one guide in plain English

GAIA shows you what needs your decision — and quietly handles the rest. You will never dig for a number again.

Your first 5 minutes

  1. Open GAIA Portal. The four cards at the top tell you the money story: expected rent, what's been received, what's unpaid, and the whole portfolio position.
  2. Look at the three queues below: arrears (red), overdue tasks (orange), expiring leases (blue). Red first. That's your to-do list.
  3. Click the calendar — every rent date and lease end for the month is already there.
  4. Open the timeline — your whole portfolio as bars on a timeline, grouped by estate.
  5. That's it. Everything else waits for your PA's Friday summary.

A normal day for you

  1. Glance at the Portal cards (2 minutes).
  2. Anything in a red banner needs a decision — approve, instruct, or ask the PA to draft it.
  3. Check the escalation queue: items Awaiting Principal Validation are waiting for your sign-off.

Your weekly rhythm

Friday: your PA sends a one-page digest — new arrears, expiries, open tasks. If the numbers match the Portal (they will), you're covered.

Little things that help

Numbers on every screen come only from verified records — if a payment isn't confirmed, it isn't counted.
You are the workspace owner: only you (or people you name) can invite or remove anyone. That's by design.
Ask the AI anything — plain English works: “Which estates have arrears?”
