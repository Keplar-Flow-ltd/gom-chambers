Why GAIA exists

Three slips every property practice knows.

These aren't hypothetical — they're the everyday failures GAIA was built against. Each one has a real, working answer in the system.

THE MISSED RENEWAL

A lease ends next week — and nobody flagged it

Renewals remembered by memory slip until the tenant hands in notice. By then, the negotiating room is gone.

GAIA's answer
Every lease carries an automatic 30/60/90-day flag. Calendars, timelines and the Legal desk sort themselves by urgency — 🚨 at 30 days, ⚠️ at 60, ℹ️ at 90. Nobody remembers anything; the system does.
THE PHANTOM ₦200,000

"The money has landed" — has it?

A transfer screenshot arrives. Someone believes it. Totals drift from reality, and month-end becomes an archaeology dig.

GAIA's answer
Only verified money counts. One click by the PA — checked against the bank — and every balance, chart and dashboard updates in the same second. Try it live below.
THE FIELD BLACK HOLE

"Burst pipe at A-04" — sent to the wrong WhatsApp group

Field issues live in voice notes and good intentions. Nothing is dated, owned, or traceable — until it's a dispute.

GAIA's answer
Ten seconds, phone to triage. The field agent logs an incident with a photo; it lands classified, dated and owned in the PA's queue. Evidence builds itself.

What comes in the box

Six pillars, one workspace.

Every capability below is live in the delivered system — built on Notion, so it runs anywhere the firm works, with Google where the firm already lives.

Executive dashboards

Expected rent, verified receipts, arrears and occupancy — one screen, always current, only ever counting confirmed money.

Calendars & timelines

Rent dates, lease ends and task deadlines on month grids; the whole portfolio as estate swimlanes on a timeline.

Verified money

Pending → verified workflow, per-tenant progress bars, disputed balances held separately — never silently mixed.

Tasks with owners

Field intake, PA triage, legal drafting, principal sign-off — one pipeline where nothing sits unowned and silence is visible.

Role-based access

Principal and PA hold the master data; Legal sees matters; field agents see their jobs. Invites and removals by the owner alone.

Google, flexed

Deadlines onto Google Calendar with real reminders; leases and receipts filed one-folder-per-tenancy in Drive, linked to records.
